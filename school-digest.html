Road to e-Learning

📘 Facebook 💬 WhatsApp

Carissa Primary School is Growing!

We are excited to share our journey towards becoming a digitally empowered learning environment. Whilst our e-learning programme has not officially started yet, we are hard at work laying the groundwork that will transform education at Carissa Primary School.

Our dedicated team is currently focused on building the essential infrastructure needed to bring quality digital education to every classroom. This includes connecting reliable internet access throughout the entire school premises, ensuring that every learner will have equal access to online educational resources.

The "Road to e-Learning" represents more than just technology. It's about opening doors to endless learning possibilities, preparing our learners for a digital future, and ensuring that Carissa Primary School remains at the forefront of educational excellence in our community.

What We're Working On:

  • 🌐 Installing high-speed internet connectivity across all classrooms
  • 💻 Setting up digital infrastructure and learning platforms
  • 👩‍🏫 Preparing teachers through training and development programmes
  • 📚 Curating quality digital learning resources aligned with CAPS curriculum
  • 🔧 Testing and refining our e-learning management systems

We believe that every child deserves access to the best educational tools available. As we continue this journey, we are committed to ensuring that the transition to e-learning is smooth, inclusive, and beneficial for all our learners from Grade R to Grade 7.

Stay Connected: We will keep our school community updated on our progress as we move closer to launching our e-learning programme. Thank you for your patience and support as we work towards this exciting new chapter for Carissa Primary School.

Together, we're building a brighter digital future for our learners. 🚀
